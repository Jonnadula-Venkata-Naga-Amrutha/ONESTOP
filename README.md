\# ONESTOP – Smart Local Services Platform



\## Project Overview



ONESTOP is a smart local services platform designed to connect customers with local service providers through a unified digital platform.



It aims to simplify service discovery, booking, quotation management and customer feedback while providing intelligent recommendations and administrative management features.



\## Key Features



\* User registration and authentication.

\* Local service discovery and search.

\* Service booking and availability management.

\* Quotation management.

\* Customer reviews and ratings.

\* AI-powered service recommendations.

\* Smart search and price guidance.

\* Admin dashboard and service-provider verification.

\* Complaint management.



\*Features are planned and subject to team discussion and implementation.\*
